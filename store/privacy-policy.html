Saral Money Tracker Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Saral Money Tracker ("the app") is an income and expense tracker for Android, published by Saral Techies, Amroha, Uttar Pradesh, India ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the app handles and how. In short: everything you enter stays on your phone. The app does not collect, upload, sell or share any data, and it has no internet access.

1. Data we collect

None. The app has no user accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no crash-reporting SDKs. It does not request the Android internet permission, so it cannot send data to Saral Techies or any third party. It does not read your SMS, bank apps, contact list or location.

If Android backup is turned on for your phone, Android may include the app's data in your own device backup (for example to your Google account), encrypted, like other apps' data. You control this in your phone's settings. Saral Techies cannot access it.

3. Import, export and automatic backup

When you import an Excel file or export a backup (Excel or CSV), the app reads or writes only the file you pick in Android's file picker. Exported files are saved where you choose; keep them safe, because they contain your financial records.

If you turn on automatic backup, the app saves a backup file on a schedule into the folder you choose with Android's folder picker, and keeps the last few copies there. If that folder belongs to a cloud service on your phone (for example Google Drive), that app uploads the file to your own account under its terms; Saral Money Tracker itself still sends nothing over the internet. You can turn automatic backup off, or remove the app's access to the folder, at any time.

4. Contacts, statements and reminders

When you tap "From contacts", Android's contact picker opens and the app receives only the one contact you choose (its name and phone number). The app does not have the contacts permission and cannot see your contact list.

Loan statements, account statements, reports and payment reminders are made on your phone. They leave it only when you share them yourself, through the app you pick (for example WhatsApp, SMS or email). They contain the details you entered, including your bank and UPI details if you added them; only share them with the person they are meant for.

5. Home-screen widget

If you add the widget, it shows today's and this month's spending on your home screen, where anyone holding your phone can see it. Remove the widget if you'd rather not show these amounts.

7. Links

The Rate, Share, More apps and Privacy policy items open Google Play, Android's share sheet or your own web browser. Sending a reminder on WhatsApp opens WhatsApp with the message ready for you to send. Google Play's in-app rating and update prompts are shown by the Google Play Store app.

8. Sharing and selling

We do not share, sell or rent any data, because we do not collect any.

9. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

10. Security

Your data stays in the app's private storage, protected by Android's app sandbox, and you can add the app lock and hide the app's content in recent apps.

11. Not financial advice

The app records and summarises the numbers you enter. It does not give financial, investment or tax advice, does not lend, collect or move money, and its reports are not audited statements.

12. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date. If the app ever starts collecting data, we will update this policy and the Google Play Data safety section before that version is released.
